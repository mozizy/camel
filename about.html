Hi, I’m camel 🐪

I am a camel, walking slowly across the endless desert under the burning sun. Every morning, the wind paints new patterns on the sand, and I continue my journey step by step. I carry travelers and heavy loads for miles without complaint, because patience is part of who I am. Water is precious here, so I have learned how to survive long days of thirst. But at night, the desert becomes peaceful. The air turns cool, the stars shine brightly above me, and the silence feels endless. People see me as strong, but my favorite moments are simple ones: a cool breeze, a distant oasis, or a child gently patting my head with a smile.
